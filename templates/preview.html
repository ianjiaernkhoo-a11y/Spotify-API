Monitor calibration

Browsers can't ask your monitor how many pixels-per-inch it really has, so "mm" in CSS is just a guess. Calibrate once with a real-world object and this page remembers it (stored in this browser).

  1. Grab a standard bank/credit/ID card (85.60 × 53.98 mm — nearly every wallet card is this size).
  2. Hold it up against the outline below.
  3. Drag the slider until the outline matches the card exactly.
